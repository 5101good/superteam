# Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, welcome good-faith participation, and focus review on ideas and evidence. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

The local-only `1.0.0` release does not invent an enforcement address.
Conduct reports can be accepted only through a channel published at the
project's verified public location. Until that channel exists, do not send
private reports to inferred or personal contacts. This limitation does not
weaken the behavior expected in local development or review.
